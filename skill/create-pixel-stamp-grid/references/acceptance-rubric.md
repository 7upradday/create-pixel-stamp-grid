# Acceptance rubric

Reject or revise the earliest stage that fails.

## Content

- Exactly four source subjects are represented.
- Tile order matches the supplied or confirmed order.
- Every tile remains recognizable against its own source.
- Viewpoint, main silhouette, and approximate subject placement are preserved.
- No unrequested object, person, word, logo, or decoration appears.
- Portrait tiles preserve hairstyle, face shape, pose, clothing color, and held objects without caricature.

## Pixel treatment

- All tiles use the same logical resolution unless a documented exception is necessary.
- Pixels are square and consistently sized.
- No antialiased or semi-transparent edge remains inside flat tiles.
- Subject readability survives thumbnail viewing.
- Default preserve mode retains the generated tile colors without a dominant-hue cast.
- Local mode keeps each tile's semantic accents recognizable.
- Optional shared mode creates cohesion without shifting unrelated metals, skin, fur, water, food, whites, or neutral shadows toward the background hue.

## Layout

- Canvas is 3:4 unless the user requested another format.
- Four independent stamp borders are complete and unfused.
- Every stamp has exactly 12 large semicircular scallops on each side.
- Tile sizes match exactly.
- Horizontal and vertical gutters match within two output pixels.
- Grid is horizontally centered and located in the upper-middle.
- Lower title area remains clear when no title is requested.

## Background

- Exactly two posters exist: primary-color dots and secondary-color stripes.
- The primary and secondary families come from the original-image palette report, not a fixed preset or generated-tile palette.
- Pattern contrast is lower than stamp content contrast.
- Pattern spacing is regular.
- The striped preset contains exactly 18 full-height vertical stripes.
- Background colors are luminous macaron conversions of their recorded source families.
- Background reads bright and fresh at thumbnail size, without mustard, ochre, gray-beige, or a low-contrast gray veil.
- Tile colors remain faithful to the accepted generated tiles; skin, fur, metal, water, food, dark outlines, and white highlights remain credible.
- Vintage mottling is subtle and does not enter the white stamp border or tile content.
- Background does not visually merge with the stamp border.

## Theme title

- Title contains exactly three words and 15–26 characters.
- Word one is an anchor supported by at least two images; palette anchors require three.
- Word two is a mood or season term supported by two images or a documented whole-set assessment.
- Word three is an evidence-approved experience noun.
- Title contains no brand, person, precise place, copied package text, unsupported claim, punctuation, or promotional cliché.
- Title is rendered in the lower reserved area unless the user explicitly disables it.
- Lettering uses the bundled connected calligraphic font, a background-conditioned complementary ink color in the 1.9:1–3.6:1 soft decorative contrast band, and the staggered two-line layout.
- The title is spelled exactly as validated, remains fully inside the canvas, and does not touch the stamp grid.
